MY STORY & APPROACH

Architecture first

I focus on turning SketchUp views into expressive visual concepts while respecting the original building geometry, proportions, openings, layout and camera view.

My design language

I’m drawn to minimalist contemporary architecture, thoughtful curves, natural materials and subtle Khmer-inspired identity, while keeping a clean modern character.

Why I built WRENDERFLOW

I wanted an accessible place where designers and students can explore rendering ideas, compare the original SketchUp image with the result, and communicate concepts more clearly.

Made in Cambodia

WRENDERFLOW is an independent creative project. My goal is to keep learning, improving the tool and making architectural visualization more approachable for creators everywhere.
